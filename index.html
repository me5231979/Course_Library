Full course description (printable)
Learning objectives
  1. Frame a stuck problem with CRIT (Context, Role, Interview, Task) instead of circling it alone
  2. Ask for twenty options with real range, wild ones included, rather than stopping at the first answer that sounds fine
  3. Filter the flood with criteria you own: pick, combine, and sharpen the two or three worth developing

Decisions, Sharper

Classroom 45 min · Self-paced ~15 min

Embodies an entrepreneurial spirit and leverages data and technologyArtificial IntelligenceDecision MakingCritical Thinking

Widen the view before the call: test scenarios, surface hidden assumptions, and decide with more confidence. One method, widen, weigh, decide: lay the decision out with CRIT (roles, never names), test best, expected, and worst case, run a premortem, then make the call yourself and write down why. Three worked examples (conflicting recommendations, a scenario test, an assumptions check), every claim cited, and a printable one-page plan.

  • 11 pages · 3 worked examples + knowledge check
  • About 15 minutes self-paced · one session live
  • Classroom, facilitator, and self-paced editions
Full course description (printable)
Learning objectives
  1. Lay a decision out with CRIT, describing every person by role, never by name
  2. Test the decision before it’s real: best, expected, and worst case, a premortem, and second-order effects
  3. Design the AI’s role for each decision: it informs or recommends, and it never decides
  4. Interrogate confident answers, and check any factual claim before it moves your decision
  5. Make the call yourself, keep your reasoning in the record, and disclose substantive AI help to the people who rely on it

Workflow & Process Redesign

Classroom 90 min · Self-paced ~45 min

Part of the Manager Voyage program

Embodies an entrepreneurial spirit and leverages data and technologyLeads and inspires teamsProcess MappingAI LiteracyChange Management

Redesign the work, then add the AI. McKinsey's State of AI research found workflow redesign is the strongest factor separating AI high performers from the 80-plus percent seeing no bottom-line impact, and that work happens at the manager's level. This session teaches the method: map a real team workflow into steps (gather, transform, judge, commit), assign each step with the draft-verify-decide pattern, design verification that actually catches errors, rebuild a workflow in the graded Redesign Lab, and launch a pilot your team will trust.

  • 6 sections · 7 interactive tools + the Redesign Lab
  • Classroom 90 min (60 core) · self-paced ~45 min
  • In person, virtual, or self-paced
Full course description (printable)
Learning objectives
  1. Explain why workflow redesign separates AI high performers from the crowd seeing no bottom-line impact
  2. Map one of your team's workflows into steps and separate the mechanical work from the judgment work
  3. Apply the draft-verify-decide pattern to assign every step: AI drafts, a human verifies, a human decides
  4. Design verification with teeth: a named owner, real sources, and a reject path that gets used
  5. Build a redesign card for one real workflow and commit to a piloted first step with a visible metric
